Sonder 1.7.3 checks that a book can actually download before starting it, makes downloads sturdier and easy to cancel, and tidies up Settings and the update screen.

- Tapping Download to library now asks Real-Debrid to look for people sharing the upload first. If nobody is, Sonder tells you it won't download right now and to try another time or pick another upload, instead of adding a download that never finishes. The check takes up to a minute and nothing is left on your Real-Debrid account when it fails.
- Cancel a download from the book's page, from the progress notification, or with the Cancel button in Find's download list. Cancelling deletes the partial files and stops the transfer on Real-Debrid.
- A download whose seeders all go offline now stops after 20 minutes without progress, rather than an hour.
- Downloads ride out connection trouble. If your phone goes offline, the download waits for the connection to come back (up to half an hour), and dropped connections or Real-Debrid hiccups are retried for up to ten minutes instead of stopping the download.
- Retry checks for seeders first, just like a new download.
- Sonder checks there's room on your phone before a download starts, instead of failing partway through.
- Settings shows your Real-Debrid premium end date in full, and warns in red when the token isn't accepted, the account is free, or premium ends within a week. The token box has Paste and Show buttons.
- Saved folders in Settings show their names, like "Books" instead of "primary:Books", and every setting lines up with an icon.
- Release notes on the update screen show their headings, bullets, and bold text instead of raw formatting.

Install over your existing Sonder app to keep your library and listening progress.
