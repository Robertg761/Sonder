Sonder 1.6 rewinds five seconds when you resume listening.

- Pause and play again to hear the last five seconds, using the app, lock screen, or headset controls.
- Audible notifications that temporarily take audio focus pause the book. When the interruption ends, playback resumes five seconds earlier.
- Pausing manually during an interruption keeps the book paused until you press Play.
- Rewind can cross track files and stops at the beginning of the book. Seeking or jumping to a bookmark while paused keeps your chosen position.

Install over your existing Sonder app to keep your library and listening progress.
