Sonder 1.7.3 checks that a book can actually download before starting it, and makes downloads easy to cancel.

- Tapping Download to library now asks Real-Debrid to look for people sharing the upload first. If nobody is, Sonder tells you it won't download right now and to try another time or pick another upload, instead of adding a download that never finishes. The check takes up to a minute and nothing is left on your Real-Debrid account when it fails.
- Cancel a download from the book's page, from the progress notification, or with the Cancel button in Find's download list. Cancelling deletes the partial files and stops the transfer on Real-Debrid.
- A download whose seeders all go offline now stops after 20 minutes without progress, rather than an hour.

Install over your existing Sonder app to keep your library and listening progress.
