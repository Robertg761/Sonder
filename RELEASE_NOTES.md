Sonder 1.8.2 stops the previous button from sending you back to the start of the book.

- Previous in the notification, on the lock screen, and on headphones or Bluetooth controls now only restarts the chapter you're in. It never goes back to an earlier chapter or to the start of the book. The Previous chapter button in the player still steps back a chapter when pressed at the start of one.
- Next in those places moves to the next chapter.
- After any chapter skip, the player shows where you were before it, with a Return button. Tap it to go straight back, or dismiss it. It stays until you use it, dismiss it, or listen your way back to that spot, even if Sonder is closed in between.

Install over your existing Sonder app to keep your library and listening progress.
