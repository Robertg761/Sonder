Sonder 1.8.3 lets you download a book again after deleting it, and keeps new downloads from merging into an existing book.

- A book you removed or deleted from Sonder can now be downloaded again. Before, Sonder kept saying it was already downloaded even though it was no longer in your library.
- A new download of a title you already have now shows up in Recently Added as its own book instead of adding its chapters to the old one. Books that were already merged are not split apart.

Install over your existing Sonder app to keep your library and listening progress.
