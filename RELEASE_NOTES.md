Sonder 1.5 has a new look: the fore-edge wave.

- A new app icon: two amber covers holding a page edge that is also a sound wave. It fills every launcher shape and supports Android 13 themed icons.
- The whole app takes its colors from the icon. Dark mode is ink navy with an amber accent; light mode is warm paper with burnt amber.
- The icon now appears inside the app: on an empty library, in Settings › About, and as a small waveform marking the current chapter.
- Android 12 and later open on the icon, and dark mode no longer flashes white while the app starts.
- Playback notifications show the Sonder icon in the status bar.
- Books without cover art get generated covers in the new palette.
- An empty library hides search and filters until you add a book.

Install over your existing Sonder app to keep your library. Version 1.2 and earlier need this one manual update to enable in-app updates.
